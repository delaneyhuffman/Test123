Prototype · reviewer-facing artifact

Route Override Approval — Review Card

A concrete view of how route override decisions are authorized and audited, per the PRD Role-Based Route Override Controls.

Traces to the source Product Signal on enterprise route override authority (RoboFlex, TechWare, FleetOps).

Role hierarchy & authority

Operator

Tier 1 · initiate
  • Suggest / request a reroute
  • Approve own override
  • Resolve conflicts

Supervisor

Tier 2 · authorize & prioritize
  • Approve or deny operator requests
  • Priority override on conflicts
  • Bypass safety hold

Safety Officer

Tier 3 · full override
  • Full override at any time
  • Halt / reroute any robot
  • Overrides supervisor priority

Operator → Supervisor → Safety Officer · higher tier can authorize or override lower tiers

Authorization outcome states

Requested Approved Supervisor priority override Denied Safety override

Where override settings live

Route override permission tiers are configured at Fleet Management → Operator Roles → Route Override. This prototype surfaces the path directly to address the discoverability gap raised in onboarding (settings were "not obvious at all" and required an AE walkthrough).
Static prototype — no backend, no live fleet control, no persistence. Sample data is illustrative and drawn from PRD evidence. Scope and non-goals are defined in the PRD Role-Based Route Override Controls and its source Product Signal.
